Swing-Trader Overview — 8 structural patterns

Click any to explore at full detail.

01 Newspaper Hero

Full-width treemap hero, dense macro strip above, three widget columns below. Scan → drill.

02 Bloomberg Grid

Dense 3×3 tile grid with dark topbar. Everything visible at once, no scroll. Terminal aesthetic.

03 Intelligence Brief

Three editorial columns: State (market) / Signal (heat + leaderboard) / Action (calendar, portfolio). Structured like a briefing doc.

04 Context Map

Treemap fills 2/3 of the canvas, stacked widgets on the right. The map IS the dashboard — everything else is context.

05 Momentum Pipeline

Three horizontal swimlanes: State → Signal → Action. The signal lane is taller and is the hero. Read top-to-bottom like a decision chain.

06 Inverted Stream

Dashboard panel pinned above a condensed tweet stream. Context above, evidence below. The stream remains but shrinks to a strip.

07 Concordance Split

The concordance/divergence split IS the hero. Validators on the left (chatter + price agree), contrarian setups on the right. Signals mapped to action.

08 Focus Tabs

Tab-switched focus mode. One widget fills the view at a time. Others collapse to an icon strip. Zero distraction, maximum depth on the selected signal.
